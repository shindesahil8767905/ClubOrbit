COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about joining and hosting communities on ClubOrbit.

Is ClubOrbit free to use?+

Yes! Discovering clubs, joining communities, participating in discussions, and RSVPing for regular events is 100% free forever for all members.

How do I create and lead a club?+

Click '+ Create a Club' on the Clubs page to configure your club name, category, rules, and banner. As a lead, you receive administrative privileges to manage members, pin announcements, and host events.

Can I host online events as well as in-person meetups?+

Yes, we support both formats seamlessly. When hosting an event, simply choose between Virtual (Zoom/Discord/Meet link) or In-Person (physical venue address). Members receive calendar confirmations.

How is moderation and community safety handled?+

Every club has designated moderators with tools to pin, remove spam, ban bad actors, and maintain kind, inclusive spaces. We also maintain a platform-wide code of conduct.

What tools do club leaders get?+

Club hosts get member management controls, role assigners (Admin, Moderator, Member), pinned announcements, event ticketing, and an analytics overview of discussions.
