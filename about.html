WHY WE BUILT CLUBORBIT

Belonging shouldn't be left to chance.

Traditional social algorithms reward rage-bait and doom-scrolling. ClubOrbit is designed for genuine peer connection, authentic craft learning, and intimate community spaces. No follower counts to stress over—just real people making real progress.

❤️

People over vanity profiles

Real human connection beats a polished bio. We make space for raw work-in-progress, honest vulnerability, and mutual encouragement.

🧭

Curiosity is a superpower

From vintage 35mm cameras to distributed Rust systems, every interest is celebrated. Beginners and specialists collaborate equally.

🛡️

Room and safety for everyone

Communities thrive when members feel secure, valued, and respected. We enforce clear zero-tolerance codes of conduct across all circles.
